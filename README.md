
# Fitness-Subscription-Analytics
Tableau analysis of customer retention, revenue forecasting, and CAC vs. LTV for a fitness subscription business.

## Tableau Public

[View the interactive Tableau dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/iffa.za1940/viz/fitness-subscription-analytics_17894568519760/CustomerCohort?publish=yes)

## Key Insights

**Customer Cohort Analysis:**  
Customers typically begin canceling within the first 1–3 months, with retention continuing to decline over time.

**Revenue Forecast:**  
Monthly revenue is expected to be around $235K–$300K over the next 12 months. The forecast does not show strong evidence of seasonality.

**CAC vs. LTV:**  
Neither the 2024 nor 2025 cohort recovers the approximately $1.67M CAC during the observed period. Therefore, a CAC break-even month is not reached for either cohort.
